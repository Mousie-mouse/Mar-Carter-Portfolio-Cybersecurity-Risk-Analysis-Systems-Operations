### Description

Tuesday morning, a fictional healthcare clinic's IT infrastructure was compromised by a coordinated attack. After successful phishing attempts, a malicious attachment was downloaded that encrypted critical operational files on the system and exposed patient PII. Operations were shut down and technical assistance was requested when the attackers deployed a ransom note promising decryption keys upon payment.

### The 5 W's

**Who** caused the incident?
> An organized threat group, likely a cybercrime group.

**What** happened?
> Employees at a healthcare clinic were unable to access operationally critical files and systems. The attackers displayed a ransom note demanding payment in exchange for decryption keys.

**When** did it occur?
> The incident was reported at 9 a.m., when employees were unable to access files and systems.

**Where** did it happen?
> The attack occurred within the IT infrastructure of a small U.S.-based healthcare clinic, specifically targeting operational files and regulated medical patient PII.

**Why** did it happen?
> The attackers gained access through a targeted phishing email campaign using malicious attachments. The malware encrypted files on the system and deployed ransomware.

### Additional Notes

The initial access vector, phishing email, and the impact mechanism, ransomware encryption, should be documented separately during incident analysis because they represent different stages of the attack lifecycle.
